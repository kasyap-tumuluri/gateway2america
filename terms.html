1. We are not legal professionals

This is the most important section on the page. Gateway2America is not a law firm. No one associated with Gateway2America is an attorney, a paralegal acting under attorney supervision, or a DOJ-accredited representative. Nothing on this website is legal advice, and nothing we say to you is legal advice.

What we actually do

  • We share general knowledge about how the U.S. citizenship and immigration process works.
  • We redirect you to resources — official government forms and pages, and free or low-cost legal providers.
  • We post updates about publicly announced policy changes, with a link to the original source each time.
  • We tutor, free of charge, for the civics and English portions of the naturalization test.

What we cannot and will not do

  • Give you legal advice, or advise you on your individual case.
  • Represent you before USCIS, a consulate, an immigration court, or any other body.
  • Complete, sign, review for legal sufficiency, or file forms on your behalf.
  • Assess your eligibility for any immigration benefit.
  • Predict, promise, or guarantee any outcome in your case.
  • Act for you in an emergency, including detention or removal proceedings.

No attorney–client relationship

Using this website, sending us an inquiry, or receiving a reply from us does not create an attorney–client relationship, because we are not attorneys. It does not create any other professional or fiduciary relationship, and nothing you tell us is protected by attorney–client privilege or any comparable legal protection.

When you need a real lawyer

Please consult a licensed immigration attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative if your situation involves any criminal history (including arrests without conviction, dismissed charges, or expunged records), a prior removal, deportation, or exclusion order, any period out of status or unlawful presence, a previously denied application, any concern about a misrepresentation on a past form, detention or active removal proceedings, or any deadline you may already have missed.

Free and low-cost legal help is listed here. And if you can't afford a lawyer, do tell us, because helping you track down free representation is one thing we genuinely can do for you.

2. Privacy — what we keep (almost nothing)

You're safe with us, and we don't keep information about you. We set the service up this way on purpose, so that there's almost nothing here to lose, leak, subpoena, or misuse in the first place.

What this website collects: nothing

This is a static website. It has no database, no user accounts, and no server that receives form submissions. We do not collect, log, or store your name, email address, IP address, or browsing behaviour.

How the inquiry form works

When you fill in our inquiry form and press the button, your browser opens your own email program with the text pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted anywhere at all until you press send in your own email app. Your words never pass through this website, and they never go through a third-party form service either.

This means the only copy of your inquiry is the one in your sent folder and the one that arrives in our inbox.

What we deliberately do not ask for

Our form asks three things: where you are writing from, what citizenship you currently hold, and what your question is. We never ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your date of birth
  • Your A-number or USCIS receipt number
  • Your Social Security number
  • Your passport number
  • Your street address
  • Any password, or any payment information

Please do not send these to us. If a notice you want help with contains them, cover or omit them when you describe it. If you send them anyway, we will delete the message and ask you to resend it without them.

Deletion within 7 days

Once we have answered your question and the conversation has concluded, we delete your message — and our replies — from our email account within 7 days. We do not archive inquiries, keep a case log, or maintain a record of who asked what.

One honest limitation you should understand: our email is hosted by a third-party provider (Gmail). While we delete messages from our account on that schedule, we cannot control that provider's own backup and retention practices, and we do not control your email provider's. We tell you this because overstating it would be dishonest. If your question is so sensitive that provider-level retention is a real risk to you, that is a sign you should be speaking to an attorney under privilege rather than to us.

We never sell or share anything

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone. We have no advertisers, no sponsors, and no referral arrangements. We would disclose information only if legally compelled by valid legal process — and the point of holding almost nothing is that there would be very little to give.

The assistant on this site

The Gateway Assistant chat widget runs entirely inside your own browser. Your questions to it are not sent to us, to any AI service, or to any server. Nothing you type into it leaves your device, and nothing is saved when you close the page.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. Not essential ones, not analytics ones, not advertising ones. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

We also do not use:

  • Analytics of any kind (no Google Analytics or equivalent)
  • Advertising or retargeting pixels
  • Social media tracking widgets
  • Session recording or heatmap tools
  • Fingerprinting techniques
  • Local storage or session storage to track you

We genuinely do not know how many people visit this site or which pages they read. We have accepted that trade-off deliberately.

Third-party requests

For honesty and completeness: this site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's font servers and Google may observe your IP address in doing so. We set no cookies through it and receive no data from it. If you follow an external link from our site — to uscis.gov or anywhere else — that site's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

4. Code of conduct

This applies both to us and to you. It exists because the people who write to us are often frightened, and they deserve to be treated well.

What you can expect from us

  • We treat you with dignity, regardless of your nationality, immigration status, religion, race, ethnicity, language, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
  • We do not judge you. We will not lecture you about your immigration history or your choices.
  • We answer honestly, including when the honest answer is "we don't know" or "you need a lawyer for this."
  • We never charge you, ask you for money, or accept gifts in exchange for help.
  • We do not report you to anyone. We are not affiliated with any government agency and we have no obligation or intention to share anything about you with one.
  • We stay in our lane and tell you when a question is beyond what we can responsibly answer.

What we ask of you

  • Be honest with us about your situation, so our general information is at least pointed in the right direction.
  • Do not send us personal identifiers — see the privacy section above.
  • Do not ask us to do things we have said we cannot do, such as filling in your forms or telling you what to do in your case.
  • Treat us civilly. We are volunteers. We will end conversations that become abusive, harassing, or threatening.
  • Do not ask for help breaking the law, including fabricating documents or evidence, or misrepresenting facts to a government agency. We will refuse.
  • Do not represent yourself as us. Do not use our name or recognition to imply that we endorse or vouch for your case.

In-person events

The same conduct standards apply at any in-person meetup. Please do not bring original documents you cannot replace, and do not share other attendees' information or situations with anyone afterwards. What people say at a meetup is theirs, not yours to repeat.

6. Accuracy and external links

We work hard to be accurate and we cite our sources. But immigration law and policy change constantly — sometimes within days — and we are volunteers, not a newsroom.

  • Everything here is general information, not tailored to your circumstances.

  • Always confirm against the official source before you act. We link it every time so that you can.
