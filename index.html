What we do

Four kinds of help, all of it free

A quick note before anything else: we're volunteers rather than lawyers, so legal advice is off the table for us. Pretty much everything else is fair game, though — explaining how the system works in ordinary language, pointing you at the right official resource, and sticking with you while you work through it.

Pathways

There isn't one single route to citizenship, which is part of why the whole thing feels so murky at first. We lay out the common ones — green card holder, spouse of a citizen, child, refugee or asylee, military service — so you can work out which one is yours and what tends to come next.

Your privacy

We built this so there's nothing to leak

A lot of people hesitate before asking anyone for help, because they're worried about where their information ends up, and that worry is completely reasonable given everything going on. So we set the whole service up to hold as close to nothing as we could manage.

The form doesn't ask for your name. It also doesn't ask for your date of birth, your A-number, or your passport number, and to be clear, you shouldn't send us any of those even if you want to. All we really need is a rough sense of where you're writing from, what citizenship you hold at the moment, and what you're trying to understand.

Read the full privacy terms →

  • No database, no account

    There's no login here and no stored submissions, because there's no server to store them on. Your words go from the page into your own email program.

  • Gone within 7 days

    Once your question has been answered, we delete the message from our inbox within seven days. That one's on us to keep doing, and we do.

  • No tracking, no advertising

    No analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no cookies following you around afterwards. We genuinely don't know how many people visit.

  • We never sell anything

    The help is free, so there's nothing for us to upsell you later and no real reason to hang onto your details in the first place.
