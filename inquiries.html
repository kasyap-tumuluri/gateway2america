NO

What we cannot do

Give legal advice
None of us are attorneys or accredited representatives, so we can't tell you what to do in your own particular case.
Represent you
Before USCIS, a consulate, or an immigration court.
Fill out or sign your forms
Or file any of them on your behalf.
Assess your eligibility
Or guess at whether your case is likely to be approved.
Advise on anything complicated
A criminal record, a prior removal order, time out of status, a denial, a misrepresentation concern. Those really do need a lawyer, because the stakes are too high for anything less.
Handle emergencies
If you or someone in your family has been detained, please contact an immigration attorney straight away rather than writing to us.
Expedite anything
Nobody can, so anyone telling you otherwise is trying to defraud you.
Your inquiry

Three questions, that's all

We only ask for what we genuinely need to give you a useful answer. Knowing roughly where you're writing from tells us which rules and local resources apply, and your current citizenship tells us which pathways are even open to you in the first place.

A state, or a country if you are outside the U.S. A city is fine too. We do not need your street address.

The country of your passport. If you are stateless, or hold more than one, just say so.

In your own words, in any language you are comfortable writing. Tell us what you are trying to do and where you are stuck.

Please don't send us: your full name, date of birth, A-number, Social Security number, passport number, or any kind of password. We don't need any of it, and honestly we'd rather not have it. If the notice you're asking about has those details printed on it, cover them up before you describe it to us.

Pressing the button opens your own email program with your answers filled in. You can read it over, change anything, and then send it yourself. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in your own email app.

Prefer to write directly? Email gateway2america.primary@gmail.com. If your email app does not open, that address is always the fallback.

After you send it

What happens next

1

We read it, usually the same day

A real person reads every single inquiry that comes in — there's no ticketing system here and no automated reply waiting to greet you.

2

You hear back within 24–48 hours

Written in plain language, with links to the official source so you can check everything we've told you. And if your question turns out to need a lawyer, we'll just say so clearly rather than guessing at it.

3

Ask follow-up questions freely

One answer hardly ever wraps up the whole conversation, which is completely fine by us. There's no limit on follow-ups and no charge either.

4

Then we delete it

Within seven days of helping you, your message gets deleted out of our inbox, and we keep no record anywhere of who asked what. Read the full privacy terms →
