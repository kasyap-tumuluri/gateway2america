USCIS mandates enhanced security checks across many application types

April 30, 2026Policy Update

On April 27, 2026, USCIS mandated enhanced security checks for a wide range of immigration applications, directing officers to resubmit pending cases for expanded FBI background screening.

This followed a period in which USCIS had placed holds on several categories of applications as part of broader screening changes, beginning to lift some of those holds selectively from March 30, 2026.

Separately during 2026, USCIS activated the FBI's Next Generation Identification system, which required millions of applicants' archived fingerprints to be resubmitted and temporarily halted adjudications.

What this looks like from your side

Mostly, it looks like silence. A case sitting at "case was received" or "fingerprint review was completed" for many months, with no request for evidence and no interview notice. This is the single most common question we receive, and the answer is usually that nothing is wrong with your case — it is in a queue you cannot see.

What you can do

Keep your address current with USCIS
A missed notice can mean a denial for abandonment. Update it directly with USCIS, not just with the postal service.
Respond to any request for evidence immediately
And within the deadline.
Renew documents that expire while you wait
Work authorization, travel documents, green cards.
Escalate if you are past the published processing time
A service request, then the USCIS Ombudsman, then a congressional inquiry. All free.
