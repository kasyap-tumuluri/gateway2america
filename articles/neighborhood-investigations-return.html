Neighborhood investigations return after 34 years

August 26, 2025Policy Update

A policy memorandum dated August 22, 2025 revived neighborhood investigations for naturalization applicants — an authority under section 335(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act that had not been used since 1991.

The authority permits USCIS to investigate an applicant's residence, employment, and reputation in the community, including by speaking with people who know them.

It will not happen to everyone

This is worth stating plainly, because the coverage alarmed a lot of people: USCIS has indicated it will not conduct investigations for every applicant. The authority exists and is being used in certain cases, not as a universal step.

If it happens in your case

Your own records are your protection
Leases, utility bills, pay stubs, and tax filings that corroborate the addresses and employers on your N-400 are what resolve questions.
Make sure your application is accurate first
An investigation is only a risk if what it finds differs from what you wrote.
Nobody is obliged to speak to an investigator
Including your neighbours and your employer. They may decline.
Verify identity
Officials carry credentials. This authority is an obvious opening for impersonation scams — nobody legitimate will demand payment or threaten you.

USCIS formally incorporated this into its Policy Manual in August 2026, which we covered separately.
