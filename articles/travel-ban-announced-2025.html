New travel restrictions announced for 19 countries

June 6, 2025Policy Update

On June 4, 2025, a presidential proclamation suspended the entry of certain foreign nationals from 19 countries, effective June 9, 2025.

Full restrictions — 12 countries

Afghanistan, Burma, Chad, Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Haiti, Iran, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, and Yemen. Limited exceptions apply.

Partial restrictions — 7 countries

Burundi, Cuba, Laos, Sierra Leone, Togo, Turkmenistan, and Venezuela. Restrictions here apply to specified visa categories, including F and J student and exchange visas.

The crucial carve-out

The restrictions apply to nationals of these countries who were outside the United States without a valid visa as of June 9, 2025.

If you were inside the United States on June 9, or outside but holding a visa that was valid on that date, you would generally not be covered. That distinction matters enormously and it is widely misunderstood — including, at times, by people giving advice.

If this touches your family

Do not assume a case is hopeless, and do not assume it is fine. Both errors are costly. Get the specific facts assessed: nationality, location on June 9, visa status on June 9, and visa category. Travelling without confirming your status first is the real danger here.
