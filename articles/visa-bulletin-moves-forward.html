April 2026 Visa Bulletin shows significant forward movement

April 7, 2026How It Works

The April 2026 Visa Bulletin showed significant forward movement in several categories — welcome news after a long period of stagnation.

How the Visa Bulletin works

Most green card categories are numerically limited, with per-country caps. When demand exceeds supply, a queue forms, and your place in it is your priority date — usually the date your petition was filed.

The monthly bulletin publishes two sets of dates per category and country:

Final Action Dates
When a green card can actually be approved for someone with that priority date.
Dates for Filing
When you may submit your application, ahead of approval.

USCIS announces each month which chart applies for adjustment of status, so check that alongside the bulletin itself.

Reading your own row

Find your category, then your country of chargeability. If the listed date is later than your priority date, your turn has come. "C" means current — no backlog.

Important caution

Dates move backwards as well as forwards, and retrogression is real enough that it happens fairly regularly. Forward movement in one month is not a trend, and nobody can tell you reliably when your date will be reached.

When your date does become current, act promptly — and keep your address updated with USCIS so you do not miss the notice.
