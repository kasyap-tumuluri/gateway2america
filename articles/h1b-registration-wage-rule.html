H-1B selection now weighted by wage level

March 23, 2026Policy Update

A final rule effective February 27, 2026 changed how the H-1B cap selection works. Rather than each registration having an equal chance, each now receives a number of entries based on wage level — higher offered wages receive more entries and therefore a better chance of selection.

The FY2027 registration period ran from March 4 to March 19, 2026, with the petition filing window opening April 1, 2026. USCIS also reached the H-2B cap for the second half of FY2026 on March 20, 2026.

Who this affects

Mostly employers and people on student visas moving toward work sponsorship. It disadvantages entry-level roles and favours senior positions, which has real consequences for recent graduates on F-1 status using OPT.

If you are on F-1 and hoping for H-1B

Know your OPT and STEM OPT timelines precisely
Your runway determines how many lottery cycles you get.
Ask where your offered wage sits
Relative to the prevailing wage levels for your role and location. It now directly affects your odds.
Consider cap-exempt employers
Universities, affiliated nonprofit research organizations, and some nonprofit research institutions are not subject to the cap — no lottery at all.
Look at other categories
O-1 for extraordinary ability, cap-exempt options, or country-specific routes such as TN, E-3, or H-1B1.

Work with your school's international office and your employer's immigration counsel. Timing errors here are expensive and hard to undo.
