Can you pay less for citizenship? Fee waivers and reduced fees

April 21, 2025How It Works

The naturalization fee is a real barrier, and a lot of people who qualify for help never ask for it.

Current costs

$760
Filing on paper.
$710
Filing online — $50 cheaper for the same application.

The reduced fee

If your household income is at or below 400% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, you may file for a reduced fee of $380, using Form I-942. Note that the reduced fee is available on paper filings only.

400% of the guidelines is a higher ceiling than most people assume — it reaches well into working-household income. It is worth checking the current table against your actual household size rather than guessing.

Full fee waiver

Form I-912 requests a complete waiver. You generally qualify if you receive a means-tested public benefit, your household income is at or below 150% of the poverty guidelines, or you are in financial hardship.

Get the application checked

Fee waiver requests get denied for missing documentation more than for ineligibility, and plenty of nonprofits will review yours for free. Our legal help page lists a few ways to find one.

One caution: fee policy is under active review, and a rule proposed in 2026 would remove both the reduced fee and waivers for the N-400. If you qualify now, there is an argument for not waiting.
