DHS terminates TPS for Somalia

January 16, 2026Policy Update

On January 13, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security announced the termination of Temporary Protected Status for Somalia.

Somalia had been designated for TPS since 1991 — one of the longest-running designations in the program's history. Beneficiaries include people who have lived in the United States for over three decades.

Immediate steps

Find the official notice
And note the exact dates. A termination usually comes with a wind-down period during which status and work authorization continue. Know your real last date rather than assuming the worst or the best.
Get screened for alternatives
Now, not later. After 30+ years, many people have options they have never examined — most commonly a U.S. citizen child over 21 who can petition for a parent.
Gather your history
Every TPS approval, every EAD, tax returns, proof of residence. This documentation underpins almost every alternative route.

On asylum

Asylum generally carries a one-year filing deadline from arrival, which is long past for most long-term TPS holders. There are exceptions, including changed circumstances in your home country and extraordinary circumstances in your own situation. Whether an exception applies is a genuinely legal question — get it assessed rather than assuming.
