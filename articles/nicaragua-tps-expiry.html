Nicaragua TPS designation reaches its expiration date

July 9, 2025Policy Update

The designation of Nicaragua for Temporary Protected Status was set to expire on July 5, 2025.

Nicaragua had been designated for TPS since 1999, following Hurricane Mitch. For people who had held that status for more than two decades, its end means the loss of both protection from removal and work authorization — after building an entire life here.

If you are affected

The first step is finding out what else you may be eligible for. Many long-term TPS holders have acquired other options over the years without realising it:

A U.S. citizen child who has turned 21
Can petition for a parent. This is the single most commonly overlooked route among long-term TPS holders.
A spouse
Who is a citizen or permanent resident.
Employment-based options
If an employer will sponsor.
U or T visas
If you have been the victim of certain crimes or of trafficking.
Asylum
Subject to deadline rules.

Getting a full screening from a nonprofit immigration program is genuinely worth the appointment. People routinely discover they have been eligible for something for years.

Keep every TPS approval notice, every EAD, and your evidence of continuous residence. That history is an asset.
