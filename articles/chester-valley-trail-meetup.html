Come meet us in person at the Chester Valley Trail

August 9, 2026Milestone

We are holding an in-person meetup at the Chester Valley Trail, open to anyone.

There is no agenda and no sign-up. Come with questions about the citizenship process, come to talk through where you are stuck, come for test practice, or come just to meet the people behind the emails. Bring a friend or a family member who has been meaning to ask something.

Why in person

Nearly all of our work happens over email, which is efficient but has limits. Some things are much easier face to face: looking at a notice together and working out what it is actually asking for, practising spoken English and civics answers out loud, or talking through a worry that is hard to put in writing.

There is also something to be said for discovering that other people in your area are working through the same process. Most of our inquiries begin with some version of "I don't know where to start," and it helps to learn that is the normal starting point rather than a personal failing.

What to bring, and what not to

Bring your questions, and bring along any notice you would like help reading.

Please do not bring original documents you cannot replace — your green card, passport, or original civil records. Photocopies or phone photos are fine for anything we would look at together.

Our usual rule holds in person as well: we will not ask for your full name, date of birth, or A-number, and you should not share them with us. Nothing from the conversation is written down or kept.

The trail is public and free. If you want details, or cannot make it and would like to arrange another time, send us an inquiry.
