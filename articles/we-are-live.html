Gateway2America is open

October 3, 2024Milestone

As of today, Gateway2America is active and taking inquiries.

The idea came from watching how often people get stuck on the citizenship process for reasons that have nothing to do with whether they qualify. They get stuck because a form has a name instead of a description. Because an official page is written at a reading level most people never needed to reach. Because the one question they need answered is too small to pay a lawyer for and too specific for a search engine.

That gap is what we work in. We are not attorneys and we do not give legal advice. What we offer is three things:

Answers to questions
About how the process works, usually within 24–48 hours.
Redirection
Pointing you to the correct official form, fee schedule, or free legal-aid organization instead of a paid middleman.
Citizenship test tutoring
Free, for the civics and English portions.

We also keep this articles section, where we translate the policy changes that actually affect people into plain language and link the official notice so you can verify everything yourself.

One promise before anything else: we keep no information about you. Our inquiry form does not ask your name or date of birth, and we delete your message within seven days of helping you.
