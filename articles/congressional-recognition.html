Gateway2America receives a Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition

May 19, 2026Milestone

Gateway2America has received a Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition from Pennsylvania's 6th congressional district.

A month after the Princeton Prize certificate, this recognition from our own congressional district is humbling. It is a thank-you from the place where this work happens, for work done on behalf of people who live here.

What this does and does not mean

We want to be precise, because precision matters in this field and because overstating it would undercut everything else we tell you.

This certificate is recognition of community service. It is not a federal licence, an accreditation, or an endorsement of legal services. It does not make us attorneys or accredited representatives, and it does not change what we are able to do for you.

We still cannot give legal advice. We still cannot represent you or advise on your individual case. What we do is exactly what we did before: explain the process in plain language, point you to official resources and to real legal help when you need it, tutor for the citizenship test, and post updates you can verify against the original source.

Thank you

To the constituent services staff who do quiet, unglamorous immigration casework every day — and who are often the reason a stalled case finally moves — thank you. If your case is stuck beyond the published processing time, your own representative's office can help, free of charge.
