New guidance on bringing family through Form I-730

December 27, 2024Policy Update

On December 20, 2024, USCIS issued Policy Manual guidance covering the eligibility, filing, and adjudication requirements for Form I-730, the Refugee/Asylee Relative Petition.

Form I-730 is how someone granted asylum or admitted as a refugee brings a spouse or unmarried child under 21 to join them in the United States. It is often called the "follow-to-join" petition.

The deadline people miss

There is a two-year filing deadline running from the date you were granted asylum or admitted as a refugee. It can be excused for humanitarian reasons, but that is discretionary and not guaranteed. If you have asylum or refugee status and family abroad, this deadline deserves a calendar entry today.

Relationships must exist at the right time

The qualifying relationship generally has to have existed before you were granted status — a marriage entered into afterward does not qualify under I-730. A child must be unmarried and under 21, with protections for children who age out during processing.

The consolidated guidance pulls scattered rules into one place, which makes it easier to check your own situation before filing.
