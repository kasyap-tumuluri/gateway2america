Gateway2America receives the Princeton Prize in Race Relations certificate

April 19, 2026Milestone

Gateway2America's founder, Ronit Luthra, has received a Certificate of Accomplishment in the Princeton Prize in Race Relations for the Eastern Pennsylvania and Delaware region.

The Princeton Prize in Race Relations recognises young people whose efforts have had a significant, positive effect on race relations in their schools or communities. Being recognised in the Eastern Pennsylvania and Delaware region means a great deal, because it is recognition from the community this work was built for.

What the recognition is really for

It is not for the website. It is for the hundred-plus individual conversations behind it — people who wrote in not knowing where to start, and who got a real answer in plain language within a day or two.

Immigration bureaucracy is not neutral in its effects. Whether you can navigate it depends heavily on your English, your education, your money, and whether you happen to know somebody who has done it before. People who have those advantages find the system difficult. People who do not often find it impossible. Closing even part of that gap is the point of this work.

Thank you

To everyone who wrote in, trusted us with a question, or passed our name along to a friend or a relative: this is yours as much as ours.

The work continues, and it is still free. If you have a question, the door is open — send us an inquiry.
