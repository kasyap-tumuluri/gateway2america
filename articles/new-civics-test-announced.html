USCIS announces the 2025 civics test: 128 questions, 20 asked, 12 to pass

September 22, 2025Policy Update

USCIS published a Federal Register notice on September 18, 2025 announcing implementation of the 2025 Naturalization Civics Test, applying to applications filed on or after October 20, 2025.

What changes

2008 test2025 test
Question pool100128
Questions asked1020
Needed to pass612

The officer asks questions only until you have either passed or failed.

How much is actually new

Less than the headline suggests. The 2025 test largely returns to the format of the 2020 version: more than 75% of its questions are identical to the 2020 test, roughly 25% are new, and some 2008 questions were dropped entirely.

Which test applies to you

This is determined by your filing date, not your interview date:

  • Filed before October 20, 2025 → 2008 test (10 of 100, 6 to pass).
  • Filed on or after October 20, 2025 → 2025 test (20 of 128, 12 to pass).

So if you filed in September 2025 and interview in 2026, you still take the older, shorter test. Study the right one — check your receipt notice for your filing date.

The English requirements are unchanged.
