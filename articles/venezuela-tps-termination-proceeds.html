Supreme Court allows Venezuela TPS termination to proceed

May 22, 2025Court Ruling

On May 19, 2025, the Supreme Court issued an order permitting the administration's termination of Temporary Protected Status for Venezuela to take effect while the underlying case continued in the lower courts.

An order of this kind is not a final ruling on whether the termination was lawful. It decides who bears the consequences in the meantime — and here, the practical consequence fell on TPS holders.

If you are affected

Losing TPS does not automatically mean removal, but it does mean losing protection from removal and losing the work authorization that came with it. The priority is to find out whether you have any other basis to remain:

Asylum
If you fear persecution. Note the general one-year filing deadline from arrival, and the exceptions to it.
Family-based petitions
Through a spouse, parent, or adult child who is a citizen or permanent resident.
Employment-based routes
Or U/T visas if you have been a victim of certain crimes or trafficking.
Pending applications
You may already have that carry their own protections.
