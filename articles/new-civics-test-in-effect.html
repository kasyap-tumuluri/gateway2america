The 2025 civics test is now in effect

October 21, 2025Policy Update

The 2025 Naturalization Civics Test took effect yesterday, October 20, 2025. Applications filed from that date forward are tested on the 128-question pool, with 20 questions asked and 12 correct answers required.

First: find out which test is yours

Look at your receipt notice (Form I-797) and find your filing date. That date — not your interview date — decides your test. People are already studying the wrong list, so check before you spend months on it.

If you are taking the 2025 test

Download the official 128-question list and answers
From USCIS. Do not study an unofficial list; the official answers are what count.
Start earlier than you think you need to
Twelve correct out of twenty is a higher bar than six out of ten, and there is more material.
Learn out loud
It is a spoken test, and recognising an answer on paper is not the same as producing it in a room.
Check the answers that change
Near your interview: your senators, your representative, the Speaker, the President and Vice President, your governor and state capital.

If you do not pass

You get a second attempt, normally rescheduled within 60 to 90 days, and you are retested only on the part you failed.
