Where things stand: a plain summary for anyone starting now

November 1, 2026How It Works

If you are at the beginning of this process, the last two years of news is a lot to absorb. Here is the short version.

What has become harder

Waits are much longer
N-400 processing averaged 12.7 months by August 2026, with a backlog over 813,000 and approvals down sharply.
Scrutiny is broader
Expanded vetting, social media review, revived neighborhood investigations, and a holistic good-moral-character assessment.
The civics test is longer
For anyone filing on or after October 20, 2025 — 20 questions from a pool of 128, with 12 correct to pass.
Fees may rise steeply
A proposed rule would take the N-400 to $1,330 and remove fee waivers. Not yet in effect..
TPS is far more precarious
And litigation is much less likely to pause a termination.

What has not changed

Birthright citizenship stands
The Supreme Court confirmed it 6–3 in June 2026.
The eligibility rules are the same
Five years as a permanent resident (three if married to a citizen), continuous residence, physical presence, good moral character, English and civics, the oath.
The English standard is basic English
Not fluency — and age-based exemptions still exist.
Current fees still apply
Until a final rule says otherwise, including the $380 reduced fee and waivers.
Honesty still protects you
More than anything else.

What to do first

  1. Confirm you are eligible — count your years, your trips, your physical presence.
  2. Build your document file before you fill in anything.
  3. Check fees and the current form edition on uscis.gov the week you file, and apply for a reduced fee or waiver if you qualify.
  4. File online if you can — it is cheaper and easier to track.
  5. Start studying the right version of the test for your filing date.
  6. Get a real attorney if anything in your history is complicated.

And if you are not sure where you fit in any of this, just ask us. "I don't know where to start" is the most common message we get, and it is a perfectly good place to begin.
