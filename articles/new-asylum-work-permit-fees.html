New fees for asylum applications and work permits

July 26, 2025Policy Update

Fees required by the 2025 reconciliation legislation took effect for applications postmarked on or after July 22, 2025.

The new amounts

Asylum application (Form I-589)
$100 when you file, plus $100 every year the application remains pending.
Work permit (Form I-765)
For asylum applicants, TPS holders, and parolees: $550.
Asylum work permit renewal or extension
$275.

These cannot be waived

This is the hardest part. The asylum fee and the asylum work permit fee cannot be waived or reduced, regardless of financial circumstances. Given that asylum cases routinely stay pending for years, the annual charge compounds.

Filing with the wrong fee means rejection

USCIS has been explicit that applications submitted with incorrect fees are rejected. A rejection is not a correction notice — your filing does not count, and for asylum, where a one-year deadline from arrival applies, a rejection that pushes you past that deadline is a serious problem.

Verify the exact amount on the USCIS fee page the day you file.

If you cannot afford these fees, talk to a nonprofit legal provider before abandoning a claim. Some organizations have hardship funds, and the shape of your case may offer options.
