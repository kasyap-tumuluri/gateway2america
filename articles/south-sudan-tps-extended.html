South Sudan TPS automatically extended six months

May 7, 2025Policy Update

Temporary Protected Status for South Sudan, which had been set to expire on May 3, 2025, was automatically extended for six months — from May 4, 2025 through November 3, 2025.

What an automatic extension does

When TPS is extended, existing beneficiaries keep protection from removal and keep work authorization through the new end date. Automatic extensions often also extend the validity of existing Employment Authorization Documents even when the date printed on the card has passed — the Federal Register notice is what establishes that, and it is the document to show an employer alongside your card.

What to do

Re-register during the registration period
Protection continues only if you re-register when USCIS tells you to. Missing that window is the most common way people lose TPS they were entitled to keep.
Print the Federal Register notice
If your EAD shows an expired date but is auto-extended, that notice is your proof for Form I-9 purposes.
Look beyond TPS
TPS is temporary by design and does not lead to a green card on its own. If you have another possible route — family, employment, asylum — it is worth getting it assessed.

TPS designations have been changing frequently and in both directions. Always confirm your country's current status on the USCIS TPS page rather than relying on an older article.
