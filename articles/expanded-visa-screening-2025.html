State Department expands screening and vetting for visa applicants

June 24, 2025Policy Update

On June 18, 2025, the State Department announced expanded screening and vetting for visa applicants. USCIS separately issued Policy Manual guidance strengthening the screening of family-based immigrant visa petitions.

Together these mean more review, more requests for evidence, and longer timelines across consular processing and family petitions.

What to expect

Longer waits
Including after an interview that seemed to go well. Administrative processing can add weeks or months.
More documentation requests
Sometimes for records from years earlier.
More attention to online presence
And to consistency between your application and everything else about you on record.

How to prepare

It helps to build your own file before anyone asks for it. Keep every prior application and approval notice, along with a dated travel history. Keep proof of the relationship in a family case — not just a marriage certificate, but the ordinary evidence of a shared life: a joint lease, joint accounts, photographs with dates, messages.

Make sure the story your documents tell matches the story your forms tell. Most problems at this stage are not fraud; they are a sloppy date or a forgotten trip that looks like concealment.

If you are planning travel around a pending case, build in far more time than the posted estimate.
