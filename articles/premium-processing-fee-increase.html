Premium processing fees rise March 1, 2026

March 4, 2026Policy Update

A Department of Homeland Security final rule increased USCIS premium processing fees effective March 1, 2026.

What premium processing is

Premium processing (Form I-907) buys a faster decision on certain petitions — USCIS commits to acting within a set number of business days. It applies mainly to employment-based petitions such as the I-129 and certain I-140 categories.

What it does not do

Two misunderstandings cost people money:

It does not improve your chances
You are buying speed, not favour. A weak petition gets denied faster.
It is not available for the N-400
There is no way to pay to expedite a naturalization application. If anyone offers to, it is a scam.

Expedite requests are different, and free

Separately from premium processing, USCIS has an expedite request process that costs nothing. The criteria are narrow — severe financial loss to a company or person, emergencies and urgent humanitarian situations, nonprofit interests, government interests, or clear USCIS error. Requests need real documentation, and most are denied, but the request itself is free.

If your case is simply taking longer than the published time, the free routes are a service request, the USCIS Ombudsman, and a congressional inquiry through your representative — not a payment.
