Changes proposed to the disability exception form, N-648

September 2, 2025Policy Update

On August 29, 2025, USCIS announced proposed revisions to Form N-648, the Medical Certification for Disability Exceptions.

What N-648 does

A licensed medical professional certifies that an applicant cannot meet the English and/or civics requirements because of a physical or developmental disability or mental impairment. It is the route to naturalization for people who are otherwise eligible but cannot satisfy the testing requirements.

What was proposed

  • Disqualifying applicants whose condition has not lasted 12 months.
  • Disqualifying conditions resulting directly from illegal drug use.
  • Eliminating the oath waiver question.
  • Requiring the medical professional to explain explicitly the nexus — the causal link between the disability and the inability to meet the requirements.

Pushback

The Immigrant Legal Resource Center submitted a comment opposing the revisions, arguing they conflict with existing statute, regulation, and the Policy Manual, and that the oath-waiver questions ask doctors to answer things outside their expertise.

If you are preparing an N-648

The nexus explanation is already where these are won or lost. A form that states a diagnosis without explaining why it prevents learning the material tends to be rejected. Give your doctor the time and context to write it properly, and have an accredited representative review it before filing.

Where this information comes from

  • USCIS Policy Manual — Volume 12, Part E, Chapter 3
