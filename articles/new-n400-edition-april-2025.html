New N-400 edition takes effect April 4, 2025

April 8, 2025Policy Update

USCIS issued a new edition of Form N-400 dated January 20, 2025. The effective date, originally immediate, was moved to April 4, 2025. The revision removed the "X" gender marker option that the prior edition had included.

The practical risk: filing an outdated form

This is the most common way a straightforward application gets rejected, and it is entirely avoidable. USCIS accepts only the current edition of a form, and an outdated edition can come back unfiled — costing you months.

Two habits protect you:

Download the form fresh from uscis.gov the week you file
Never fill out a PDF you saved months ago, and never use one from a third-party site.
Check the edition date
In the bottom corner of the form against the date shown on the official form page.

Check the fee at the same time

Fees change independently of form editions, and the wrong fee also causes rejection. Confirm the current amount on the USCIS fee calculator the same day you write the check.

If you were midway through a paper application when the edition changed, start again on the current form. Transcribing is tedious; a rejection is worse.
