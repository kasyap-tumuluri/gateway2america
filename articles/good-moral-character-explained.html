"Good moral character": what it means and what it covers

March 17, 2025How It Works

Naturalization requires a finding of good moral character, normally over the five years before you file (three for spouses of citizens), though officers may look further back.

It is wider than a criminal record

Some things are permanent bars — murder, and aggravated felonies as immigration law defines them. Others bar you during the statutory period: certain crimes, providing false testimony for an immigration benefit, habitual drunkenness, illegal gambling, polygamy, smuggling.

And then there are the ones people genuinely do not expect:

Unpaid taxes
Or unfiled returns.
Unpaid child support
Failure to register for Selective Service
For men who lived here between 18 and 26.

These are frequently fixable. A payment plan with the IRS, a current child support arrangement, or a Selective Service status information letter can resolve them — but resolve them before you file, not at the interview.

The 2025 shift

An August 2025 policy memorandum moved officers away from a checklist and toward a broader, holistic judgment of conduct over time, including positive contributions to the community rather than only the absence of bad acts. In practice that means evidence of community ties, steady work, and volunteer history can help, and it means outcomes are less predictable than before.
