USCIS clarifies who decides when permanent resident status is rescinded

December 16, 2024Policy Update

On December 13, 2024, USCIS issued Policy Manual guidance clarifying jurisdiction over the rescission of lawful permanent resident status.

Rescission is the process of undoing a grant of permanent residence — treating the approval as though it should not have been given. It is distinct from removal proceedings, and distinct from abandoning status by living abroad.

The guidance addresses which body has authority to act in which circumstances: when the matter sits with USCIS, and when it belongs to the immigration courts. This is procedural rather than substantive. It does not change the grounds on which status can be rescinded; it clarifies the path a case travels.

What to take from it

For most permanent residents this changes nothing about daily life. It matters if you have received a notice questioning your green card approval — in which case the identity of the deciding body affects your deadlines, your procedural rights, and where you file a response.

If you receive any notice of intent to rescind, treat it as urgent and get real legal representation. This is not a situation to navigate from a website, including ours. Our legal help page lists free and low-cost options.
