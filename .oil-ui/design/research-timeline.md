## Standing reference facts

- **N-400 fee (current):** $760 paper / $710 online. Reduced fee $380 for household
  income at or below 400% of Federal Poverty Guidelines (paper filing only).
- **Civics test — which version applies:** filed *before* 2025-10-20 → 2008 test
  (10 questions drawn from 100, 6 correct to pass). Filed *on/after* 2025-10-20 →
  2025 test (20 questions drawn from 128, 12 correct to pass). Officer stops asking
  once the applicant has passed or failed.
- **2025 test content:** returns to the 2020 format; >75% of questions identical to
  the 2020 version; ~25% new content; some 2008 questions removed.
- **English requirement:** unchanged — read one sentence, write one sentence, plus
  speaking/understanding assessed throughout the interview. No separate speaking test.
- **Age/residence exemptions:** 50/20, 55/15 (English exempt, civics in native
  language), 65/20 (English exempt + reduced civics set).
- **N-648** = Medical Certification for Disability Exceptions (waives English/civics).
- **Green card renewal:** Form I-90 receipt notice auto-extends validity **36 months**
  (raised from 24, notices began 2024-09-10). Carry green card + I-797 receipt together.
